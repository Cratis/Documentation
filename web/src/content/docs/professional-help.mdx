Professional help for Cratis is a private, paid engagement for work that needs agreed scope and dedicated time, such as an architecture review or a team workshop. Community help is open to everyone and best-effort, with no guaranteed response time. You do not need a paid engagement to ask a question.

Choose dedicated help when confidential context, a deadline, or a written deliverable matters. [Work with us](/work-with-us/) describes the work you can discuss with the maintainers and what to include in an inquiry. Scope, availability, response targets, and escalation depend on the signed agreement.

## Community help compared with dedicated help

| | Community | Dedicated |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Paid, scoped per engagement |
| Response | Best-effort, with no guaranteed response time | Response targets and escalation defined by the signed agreement |
| Context | Public; share only what you can share openly | Confidentiality and access arrangements agreed before sharing code or domain detail |
| Shape | Questions and answers, design discussion | Workshops, reviews, pairing, focused development, training |
| Output | A conversation that may lead to an issue or documentation change | An agreed deliverable, such as a model, a plan, a written review, or a first slice |
| Depth | The context you can share in a public conversation | The system boundaries and questions included in the agreed scope |

## When dedicated help is worth it

A public thread works well when you can explain the problem openly and wait for someone to respond. A private review is more appropriate when understanding the decision requires confidential code, schemas, or business context. Agree how that material will be shared before sending it.

Dedicated time also helps when several people need to work through the same model, or when a decision needs a written review. A workshop can focus on the team's questions; a review can examine consistency boundaries, tenancy, storage, or migration risks within an agreed scope. Neither is a promise to assess every part of the system.

If you only need an explanation or help with a reproducible problem you can share publicly, start with the community.

<Aside type="note" title="Commercial work is separate from community help">
Discord remains open and best-effort, and asking there never depends on being a customer. Dedicated work is handled separately, with its scope and terms agreed in writing. Availability and the relevant consultancy, employer, and conflict-of-interest arrangements may limit an engagement.
</Aside>
