Cratis writes comparisons to help you choose, not to win. Each page names the product versions it compares, the date of its review, and a first-party source for every claim about another product. Where we cannot find a fact in a product's own documentation, the page says "Not publicly documented."

The Cratis team writes these pages, and Chronicle is our product. That is a conflict of interest, and the method below is how we limit it.

## How we write them

- **Named versions and dates.** Every table records the version of each product and the date we checked it. A row describes those versions only.
- **First-party sources.** A claim about another product links to that product's documentation, repository, license or package registry. A claim about Chronicle matches the Chronicle documentation and README.
- **Capabilities, not ratings.** We list what is documented. We do not score products, rank them, or call one better.
- **Honest limits.** Each page says what it does not establish, and the Marten page has a section on where the other product is the better choice.
- **Different layers.** Products differ in what they are, for example a library against a server. The tables name which product owns each concern rather than pretending they are equivalent.

## Corrections and refresh

We recheck a page when a compared product ships a relevant major or minor release or changes its license, and at least every 90 days. If a statement cannot be confirmed from a product's current public sources, we correct or remove it.

If you find an error or a better source, [send the correction](/feedback/).
